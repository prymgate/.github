# PrymGate

PrymGate automates paid Telegram channels. A subscriber pays in USDT and gets a private invite link that only works for them. They receive a renewal reminder 7 days before their access ends, and if they don't renew, they're removed from the channel automatically. Creators get an alert for every payment, set up their bot without BotFather, and keep their member payments with 0% platform commission. PrymGate is available in English and Arabic and is free to start, with no card required.
